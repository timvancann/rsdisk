# Visuals Implementation Plan

**Goal:** the right-hand pane draws the current folder as a treemap or a sunburst (`v`),
coloured by file kind or age (`a`), in sync with the list.
**Architecture:** Layouts are pure functions from the tree to rectangles or arc segments,
tested without a window. One gpui element paints whichever layout is active with `canvas`
(quads for the treemap, `PathBuilder` arcs for the sunburst) and maps mouse positions back to
nodes. Folder colours come from a one-off summary pass in core.
**Tech Stack:** gpui-kit 0.7.3 (gpui-pre 0.3.9: `canvas`, `paint_quad`, `PathBuilder`,
`paint_path`, `shape_line`), rsdisk-core.
**Spec:** `spec.md` §2.10, §3.6, §3.8, §3.9 (`v`, `a`, mouse), §3.12 (remembered visual).

## Global Constraints

- The visual always shows the browser's current folder; selection and hover are by node,
  shared with the list (§3.8).
- Draw down until a cell would be under 4 px (treemap) or under about 1 degree (sunburst),
  and merge each folder's remainder into one neutral "(other)" cell (§3.8). The sunburst
  shows at most 6 rings, the treemap labels only its top two folder levels (both learned in
  the mockup, `mockup/index.html`).
- Layouts are computed when the folder, pane size, visual or a tree change asks for it, never
  per frame.
- Tim's UI rules apply (no gradients, no left-edge bars, no emoji, no interpuncts). The age
  ramp is the mockup's: teal (recent) through sand to red (5 years or more), log scale.
- Gate before each commit as in plan 03; screenshot with `RSDISK_NO_FOCUS=1` as in plan 03's
  rulings.

## Plan sequence (this is Plan 4 of 5)

Needs plan 03. Plan 05 (marks, Trash, search, rescans) follows and will reuse this pane's
hit testing for marking from the visual.

### Task 1: Folder summaries in core

**Files:** modify `crates/core/src/tree.rs` (or a new `summary.rs`), `spec.md` §3.6.
**Interfaces:** after the tree is built, every directory gets `kind` = the kind holding the
most bytes below it (Other when it holds no files) and `mtime` = the size-weighted mean
modified time of the files below it. One reverse pass over the arena with a temporary
`[u64; 8]` per directory; `.app` bundles keep App. Update §3.6, which says folders have no
kind of their own.
**Tests:** dominant kind by bytes, not by count; ties are stable; an empty folder is Other;
weighted age with two files of different sizes; `.app` keeps App; removed nodes (plan 05)
don't count once `remove` re-runs the summary for the ancestors.
**Steps:** tests first, implementation, gate, commit.

### Task 2: Colours

**Files:** create `crates/app/src/colour.rs`.
**Interfaces:** `fill(node, mode, theme, now) -> u32`: kind mode uses the theme's kind
colour; age mode maps days since `mtime` onto the ramp (1 day to 1825 days, log scale,
clamped); `(other)` cells and nodes without a date use the theme's faint colour. `now` is
passed in.
**Tests:** each kind maps to its theme slot; age at 1 day, 1 year and 10 years hits teal, a
mid colour and red; no date is neutral.

### Task 3: Treemap layout

**Files:** create `crates/app/src/layout/treemap.rs` (and `layout/mod.rs`).
**Interfaces:** `treemap(tree, folder, bounds) -> Vec<Cell>` where `Cell { node:
Option<NodeId> (None for an "(other)" remainder), parent, depth, rect, header: bool }`.
Squarified tiling (Bruls, Huizing, van Wijk), children largest first; folders at depth 1
and 2 get a 16 px header strip for their label, deeper folders a 1 px inset; children whose
cell would be under 4 px on either side are merged into one remainder cell per folder.
**Tests:** cells of one parent tile it exactly (area sums equal, within rounding); every
cell lies inside its parent; areas are proportional to size; aspect ratios stay under 4
for a mixed set; tiny children merge into one remainder; an empty or zero-size folder gives
no cells; a single child fills its parent.

### Task 4: Sunburst layout

**Files:** create `crates/app/src/layout/sunburst.rs`.
**Interfaces:** `sunburst(tree, folder, radius) -> Vec<Arc>` with `Arc { node:
Option<NodeId>, depth, start, end (radians), inner, outer }`. The centre disc is the folder
itself; up to 6 rings; a child's angle is proportional to its size within its parent's
span; children under about 1 degree merge into one remainder arc.
**Tests:** a ring's children cover exactly the parent's span; angles are proportional;
nothing beyond ring 6; tiny children merge; `hit(arcs, point)` returns the arc under a
point, the centre for the middle and nothing outside.

### Task 5: The pane

**Files:** create `crates/app/src/visual.rs`; modify `view.rs`, `list_view.rs`,
`settings.rs` use.
**Interfaces:** an element in the right-hand pane that paints the active layout with the
colours of Task 2, labels cells that fit (name and size, truncated with an ellipsis), draws
the selected node with an outline in the text colour, and shows a hover tooltip (path, size,
files, kind, age). Mouse: click selects that node in the list (`Browser::jump_to`); a
double-click on a folder opens it; clicking the sunburst centre or a treemap header of the
current folder goes up. `v` switches treemap and sunburst, `a` switches kind and age; both
are saved to settings right away. A small legend under the pane names the colours of the
current mode.
**Tests:** none beyond Tasks 1 to 4; this task is painting and wiring.
**Verify:** screenshots of `~` and `~/repos` in both visuals and both colour modes, light and
dark (switch the system appearance only with Tim's OK); ask Tim to try clicking and `v`/`a`.
Check that moving the selection with `j`/`k` stays smooth on `~` (layouts are cached, so a
selection change only repaints).

## Rulings during execution

- Task 3: the "too small" test is by area (under 36 px², about 6 by 6) rather than by side,
  because areas are known before tiling and sides are not.
- Task 4: 5 rings plus the centre disc, so 6 levels including the folder itself.
- Task 5, clicks: a single click selects the top-level item the cell lies under, so the list
  doesn't jump into a deep folder on every click; a double click goes to the clicked node
  itself (opening it when it's a folder). Clicking the sunburst centre goes up.
- Task 5, repaint: the pane only learns its size while painting, and a `notify` from inside
  paint is dropped, so the canvas schedules one on the next frame
  (`window.on_next_frame`). Without it the sunburst stayed empty until the mouse moved.
- Screenshot runs: `RSDISK_SETTINGS` points settings at a scratch file, and with
  `RSDISK_NO_FOCUS` the window now ignores every key. The pop-up window does become key, and
  during one run a stray `a` from Tim's typing switched the colour mode.
- Verified by screenshots: treemap of `~/repos` (kind) and `~` (age), sunburst of `~/repos`
  (kind), selection outline in both. Not verified: dark mode, hover tooltips, clicking,
  `v`/`a` live, and j/k smoothness on `~`; those need Tim.
